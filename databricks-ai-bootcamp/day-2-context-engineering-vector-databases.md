# Day 2: Context Engineering and Vector Databases on Databricks

## Focus

Design high-quality context for AI systems and create a vector-search layer on Databricks.

## What you will cover

- The role of context engineering in reliable AI applications.
- Preparing, chunking, and enriching source documents.
- Embeddings and semantic retrieval fundamentals.
- Creating and querying a Databricks Vector Search index.
- Evaluating whether retrieved context is relevant, current, and grounded.

## Practical outcome

By the end of the day, you will have a searchable knowledge corpus that can provide grounded context to an AI application.

## Suggested agenda

1. Define the questions the application should answer.
2. Prepare documents for retrieval and choose a chunking approach.
3. Generate embeddings and build a Vector Search index.
4. Retrieve and inspect relevant passages for example questions.
5. Connect retrieval results to a prompt template for a grounded response.
